# ANVAYA

## AI-Powered Smart Logistics & Accessibility Intelligence Platform

ANVAYA is an AI-powered logistics and accessibility intelligence
platform initially designed for the North Eastern Region (NER) of India.

## Smart India Hackathon

**Problem Statement:** SIH26002

**Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)

**Theme:** Transportation & Logistics

---

## Vision

To build an intelligent logistics network that can analyze routes,
predict risks, estimate travel time, understand accessibility,
and dynamically recommend safer, faster, and cost-effective
transportation routes.

---

## Core Features

- Smart route planning
- AI-based route ranking
- Cargo-aware routing
- Vehicle-aware routing
- ETA prediction
- Weather-aware logistics
- Flood and landslide risk analysis
- Dynamic route re-routing
- Accessibility intelligence
- NER accessibility heatmap
- Logistics hub intelligence
- Warehouse suitability analysis
- Logistics cost estimation
- Fuel consumption estimation
- CO₂ estimation
- Government intelligence dashboard
- Driver/mobile application
- Crowdsourced road-condition reporting
- Emergency reporting
- AI assistant
- Notifications and alerts
- Offline route support
